## VOODOO

**Nom :** VOODOO
**Développeur :** HUMPIRE / DJBILBOX BEATS
**Catégorie :** Instruments > Synthesizers > World / Physical modeling
**Type :** Instrument (VST3, Standalone)
**Version :** 1.0.0
**Description courte :** Mystic synth with 8 physical-modeling engines (flute, sitar, oud, kalimba, erhu, choir) and 70 presets. VST3 / Standalone.

**Description longue :**

VOODOO is a mystic synthesizer built on physical and modal modeling, with no samples. Eight engines cover wind, plucked, bowed-style and vocal colours: Flute, Pan Flute, Shakuhachi (breath noise plus waveguide), Sitar, Oud (Karplus-Strong with jawari buzz and sympathetic strings), Kalimba (inharmonic modes), Erhu and Voodoo Choir (saws with formants). Five timbre controls reshape each engine (breath/vowel, body/resonance, brightness, width/detune, texture). The LFO routes to pitch, filter, amplitude or timbre; the effects section adds reverb, chorus, distortion and a ping-pong delay. 12 voices, mono/poly, glide, pitch-bend and mod wheels (CC1). 70 factory presets in 7 banks of 10; user presets save as .voodoopreset. Resizable interface. VST3 and standalone.

**Nouveautés (Whats new) :** Initial release 1.0.0. 70 factory presets, 12 voices, VST3 and standalone builds.

**Tags :** oriental vst, world instruments, physical modeling, sitar vst, oud vst, flute vst, vst3, synthesizer, juce, fl studio

**Features :**
- 8 physical/modal engines, no samples
- 12 voices, mono/poly, glide
- 5 timbre controls per engine
- LFO to pitch/filter/amp/timbre
- Reverb, chorus, distortion, ping-pong delay
- 70 factory presets, user presets (.voodoopreset)
- Resizable GUI (500-2000 px wide)

**Moteurs / sources sonores :** Flute, Pan Flute, Shakuhachi (breath + waveguide), Sitar, Oud (Karplus-Strong, jawari + sympathetic strings), Kalimba (inharmonic modes), Erhu, Voodoo Choir (saws + formants)
**Banques de presets :** Flutes, Raga & Strings, Africa, Latin America, Mystic Pads, Bass & Plucks, Textures
**Contrôles :** LFO (source, destination Pitch/Filter/Amp/Timbre), pitch, glide, filter (LP/HP/BP), ADSR, 5 timbre controls, reverb, chorus, distortion, delay (time, feedback, ping-pong), mono/poly, pitch-bend and mod wheels
**Système requis :** Windows 64-bit; VST3-compatible host (e.g. FL Studio) or standalone application. No sample library to install (synthesis only).
**Formats :** VST3, Standalone
**Licence / prix :** à saisir (non défini dans le repo)

## WARZONE808

**Nom :** WARZONE808
**Développeur :** HUMPIRE / DJBILBOX BEATS
**Catégorie :** Instruments > Synthesizers > Bass / 808
**Type :** Instrument (VST3, Standalone)
**Version :** 1.0.0
**Description courte :** 808 and bass synth with unison, filter envelope, mono legato and 70 presets. VST3 / Standalone.

**Description longue :**

WARZONE808 is an 808 and bass synthesizer for trap, hip-hop and bass music. PolyBLEP oscillators feed a TPT filter with its own envelope (depth and A/D/S) and velocity sensitivity; unison goes up to 7 voices with adjustable detune; mono mode has legato glide and falls back to the note still held. 16 voices, pitch range of ±12 semitones, reverb, chorus, distortion and delay. 70 presets: 808 Subs, Dirty & Reese, Leads, Plucks & Keys, Pads & Ambience, FX & Percussion, plus the Expansion Vol.1 pack (10 presets). The EXPANSION cassette loads additional .wz808exp packs. User presets save as .wz808preset. Resizable interface. VST3 and standalone.

**Nouveautés (Whats new) :** Initial release 1.0.0. 70 factory presets, 16 voices, VST3 and standalone builds.

**Tags :** 808 vst, bass synth, reese bass, trap bass, sub bass, unison synth, vst3, synthesizer, juce, fl studio

**Features :**
- PolyBLEP oscillators, TPT filter
- Unison up to 7 voices with detune
- Filter envelope (depth, A/D/S) and velocity
- Mono legato with note-stack fallback
- 16 voices, +/-12 semitone pitch
- 70 presets incl. Expansion Vol.1; .wz808exp expansion loader
- Resizable GUI

**Moteurs / sources sonores :** PolyBLEP oscillators, TPT filter, up to 7-voice unison, filter envelope, velocity sensitivity, mono legato mode
**Banques de presets :** 808 Subs, Dirty & Reese, Leads, Plucks & Keys, Pads & Ambience, FX & Percussion, Expansion Vol.1
**Contrôles :** LFO, pitch (±12 st), glide, filter, resonance, filter envelope depth and A/D/S, velocity, unison and detune, ADSR, reverb, chorus, distortion, delay, mono/poly

**Version :** 1.0.0

**Description longue :**

OCCITANIE is a synthesizer of Occitan and European folk instruments, built without samples. Eight engines: Cabrette (nasal reed with breath), Vielle (bowed strings with buzz), Galoubet (recorder flute), Mandoline (Karplus-Strong with tremolo), Guitare, Accordéon (three reeds with musette detune), Choir and Carillon (inharmonic modes). A DRONE layer an octave below can be added to any engine for bagpipe or hurdy-gurdy textures. Filter (LP/HP/BP), ADSR, reed/body/bright/drone/velocity controls, width and texture, LFO, glide, mono/poly, reverb, chorus, distortion and delay. 16 voices. 70 presets in 7 banks of 10 with Occitan names (Capitole Cabrette, Canal du Midi, Montségur...). User presets save as .occpreset. VST3 and standalone.

**Nouveautés (Whats new) :** Initial release 1.0.0. 70 factory presets, 16 voices, VST3 and standalone builds.
