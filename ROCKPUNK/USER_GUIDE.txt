ROCKPUNK GUITAR - User Guide
DJBILBOX BEATS - version 1.1.0
================================================================================

Rock'n'roll electric guitar: two fully modelled strings, the "Urban Slayer" amp,
an LFO, a resonant filter, a 4-flavour drive, a tempo-syncable delay, a reverb
and a chorus. 76 factory presets.

ROCKPUNK ships no recording: every note is calculated. There is nothing extra
to download, and an optional layer plays YOUR OWN audio files if you drop some
in (see section 4).


1. INSTALLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Windows
  1. Run ROCKPUNK_1.1.0_Installer.exe.
  2. Accept the Windows elevation prompt: the VST3 folder is under
     "Program Files", and the installer cannot write there without
     administrator rights.
  3. The plugin is installed to
     C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ROCKPUNK.vst3

macOS
  1. Copy ROCKPUNK.vst3 to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and, if you use Logic,
     ROCKPUNK.component to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (or use the
     installer package of your download if it comes with one).
  2. The first time, macOS asks for confirmation because the plugin is not
     notarised: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

After installing, restart your DAW and force a plugin rescan:
  - FL Studio:    Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins
  - Ableton Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan
  - Reaper:       Preferences > VST > Re-scan


2. WHERE ARE THE FILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  VST3 plugin (Windows)   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ROCKPUNK.vst3
  VST3 plugin (macOS)     /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ROCKPUNK.vst3
  Presets (factory+yours) Documents\DJBILBOX\ROCKPUNK\Presets
  Your audio files        Documents\DJBILBOX\ROCKPUNK\Samples

The factory presets travel inside the plugin and are copied to your Documents
folder the first time it runs. Back up the Presets folder: uninstalling never
touches it.


3. THE INTERFACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Everything is drawn on one faceplate. The window can be resized: drag its
corner and the whole panel scales.

Mouse
  - Knobs and faders: drag up or down. Hold Shift for fine control.
  - Double-click a knob, fader or wheel to return it to its default value.
  - Mouse wheel over a knob or fader nudges it.
  - Hover over anything to read what it does.

PRESET BAR (top centre)
  - Folder button or preset name: opens the list of presets, sorted by family.
  - "<" and ">": previous / next preset.
  - Disk button: save the current sound as a preset (.rpk file).

EXPANSION cassette (top left) - click it to open a menu:
  - Init patch ................ every control back to its default value
  - Open preset file... ....... load any .rpk file
  - Save preset as... ......... same as the disk button
  - Show presets folder ....... opens Documents\DJBILBOX\ROCKPUNK\Presets
  - Delay on / Delay synced ... switch the delay, lock it to the host tempo
  - Your samples .............. the optional sampled layer (see section 4)
  - About

LFO (left panel)
  RATE      speed of the LFO (0.05 to 20 Hz)
  AMOUNT    depth of the LFO on the destination chosen with DESTINATION
  PITCH     vibrato depth, whatever the destination

VOLUME ENVELOPE (left panel faders)
  ATTACK, DECAY, SUSTAIN, RELEASE. From a dry, short rhythm sound to a pad that
  swells in.

FILTER ENVELOPE (right panel faders A S D R)
  Shapes the filter sweep. VELOCITY, the fifth fader, sets how much your
  playing dynamics shape each note (at zero every note attacks the same).

KNOBS
  GLIDE       time taken to slide from one note to the next
  FILTER      filter cut-off (turning it switches the filter on if FILTER TYPE
              is Off)
  VOLUME      master level
  REVERB      amount of reverb
  CHORUS      amount of chorus (stereo widening)
  DISTORTION  amount of drive after the amp
  TIME        delay time (turning it switches the delay on)
  FEEDBACK    number of delay repeats (turning it switches the delay on)

SPEAKER GRILLS (centre, either side of the logo)
  They are switches: click one to mute or restore the matching string. A lit
  dot at the top means the string is on. If you hear nothing, check that at
  least one grill is lit.

DROP-DOWN SELECTORS (bottom)
  WAVEFORM     how hard the pick is: Soft / Round / Bright / Sharp
  LFO SOURCE   shape of the LFO: Sine / Triangle / Square / S&H
  DESTINATION  what the LFO moves: Pitch / Filter / Volume / Drive
  FILTER TYPE  Low-pass / Band-pass / High-pass / Off
  MONO/POLY    Poly (8 notes), or Mono to play one note at a time and let GLIDE
               join them

WHEELS (bottom left)
  PITCH BEND   plus or minus two semitones, springs back to the middle
  MODULATION   opens the vibrato; it also follows MIDI CC1 from your keyboard

KEYBOARD (bottom)
  Three octaves, C2 to C5. Click and slide to play; it also lights up with the
  MIDI notes it receives.

More controls in your DAW
  Some settings have no knob on the faceplate but can be automated from the
  parameter list of your DAW: Layer Mix (crossfade between the two strings),
  Width, Limiter, each string's Blend / Tone / Body / Sustain / Pick / Level,
  the Amp (On, Gain, Distortion, Tone, Level), Drive Type and Tone, Delay
  (On, Sync, Division, Feedback, Mix, LP), Reverb Size, Filter Resonance and
  the sampled layer.


4. YOUR OWN GUITARS (OPTIONAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The sampled layer plays your files. Drop .wav, .aif or .flac files in

    Documents\DJBILBOX\ROCKPUNK\Samples

then click the cassette > Your samples and pick a file. It plays across the
whole keyboard. ROCKPUNK measures the note of each file to tune the keyboard;
if the measurement lands beside the note (chords and riffs are ambiguous), use
the "Sample Root Offset" parameter from your DAW to correct it.

A sub-folder becomes a category in the list:

    Samples/Rhythm/palm-mute-E.wav
    Samples/Solos/bend-A.wav

The folder is empty after installation, and that is normal: the 76 factory
presets only use the two modelled strings.


5. THE 76 FACTORY PRESETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

They are numbered and grouped by family. All of them are set to the same
level, so moving from one to another gives no jump in volume. None of them needs
the Samples folder.

  Crunch rhythm   14   dry attack, short notes, the heart of the product
  Leads           12   they sustain, they sing; delay and vibrato
  High gain       10   fuzz and asymmetric saturation
  Clean           12   the amp on a clean gain, chorus and delay
  Bare strings     8   no distortion, the body speaks
  Nocturnal        8   dark, closed filter, space
  Textures         8   the LFO and the filter are the subject
  Low end          4   power chords, deep body


6. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The plugin does not appear in my DAW.
  Check that the plugin scan includes C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, then
  run a full rescan.

I hear nothing.
  Make sure at least one of the two speaker grills is lit: they switch the two
  strings, and with both off there is nothing left to play. Also check that the
  plugin is not bypassed and that MIDI reaches it.

The "Your samples" list is empty.
  That is the normal state of a fresh install: ROCKPUNK ships no recording.
  Drop your files in Documents\DJBILBOX\ROCKPUNK\Samples (cassette > Your
  samples > Open the Samples folder).

Where are my presets?
  In Documents\DJBILBOX\ROCKPUNK\Presets. Back this folder up: uninstalling does
  not touch it.

Can I sell the music I make with ROCKPUNK?
  Yes, royalty-free and with no credit required. See LICENSE.txt.
