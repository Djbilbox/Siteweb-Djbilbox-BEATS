Gold bells, velvet keys, brass, organ, strings and gold sub: 70 presets from 8 engines.

HIGHROLLER GOLD, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of eight engines, shape it with five character controls (TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH, TEXTURE), then run it through a multimode filter with its own envelope, an amplitude envelope, an LFO, distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

The 70 presets are organized in seven banks of ten: bells and jackpot sounds, velvet keys, big band brass, organ, mallets and strings, gold sub and bass, and smoky pads. The ten Gold Sub & Bass presets and one brass preset load in Mono mode; the others load in Poly mode, which plays up to 16 voices.

Built for Hip-Hop, Trap and R&B keys and hooks, lounge and soul chords, brass stabs, bell toplines, pads and 808-style subs.

FAQ

Which DAWs does it work in?
It is a VST3 instrument on Windows and macOS, plus Audio Unit on macOS. It is designed for FL Studio and should load in any host that supports these formats.

Does it use samples?
No. Every sound is generated in real time by one of eight synthesis engines.

Can I play chords?
Yes. Most presets load in Poly mode with up to 16 voices. The Gold Sub & Bass presets load in Mono; switch VOICE MODE to Poly if you want chords.

Can I save my own presets?
Yes. The disk icon saves a .hgpreset file, and your presets appear in the User submenu of the preset menu.

Does it add latency?
No. The plugin reports 0 samples of latency.

Is the delay tempo-synced?
No. Delay time is set in seconds (0.05 to 1 s) and the LFO runs in Hz (0.05 to 20 Hz).
