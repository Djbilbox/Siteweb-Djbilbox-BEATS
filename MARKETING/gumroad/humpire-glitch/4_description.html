Bitcrusher from 1 to 16 bits with sample-rate reduction, speaker filter and dither.

HUMPIRE GLITCH is a lo-fi effect on a handheld-console body. The signal goes through a drive stage, a sample-rate reducer (500 Hz to 48 kHz), a dither stage and a bit quantizer from 1 to 16 bits with fractional values allowed.

The speaker switch sums to mono and passes the signal through a high-pass and low-pass filter with soft saturation. The Size knob changes the speaker from small to large. Dither can be Off, RPDF or TPDF.

The plugin adds no latency and ships with 20 presets.

Key features

  • 1 to 16 bits — Fractional bit depths are allowed.
  • Sample-rate reducer — 500 Hz to 48 kHz, with intentional aliasing.
  • Speaker filter — Mono small-speaker simulation with a size control.
  • Drive — Soft saturation before the crusher.
  • Dither — Off, RPDF or TPDF.
  • Mix and output — Dry/wet mix and -24 to +12 dB output.
  • Preset manager with A/B — Factory presets grouped by category, user presets saved by name, A/B compare, undo and redo.
  • Resizable interface — Scales from 50% to 200% with the proportions locked. Simple and Clean display modes.

FAQ

How many presets does it have?

Can I save my own presets?
Yes. The SAVE button stores the current settings under a name you choose, and they appear in the User category of the preset menu.
