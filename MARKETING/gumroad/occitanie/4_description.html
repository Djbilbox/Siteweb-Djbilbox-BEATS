Cabrette, vielle, galoubet, mandoline, accordion, choir and bells with a built-in drone: 70 presets.

OCCITANIE, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of eight engines (Cabrette, Vielle, Galoubet, Mandoline, Guitare, Accordeon, Choeur and Carillon), shape it with five character controls (REED, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE), then run it through a multimode filter, an amplitude envelope, an LFO, distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb. A DRONE control adds a soft tone one octave below each note on any engine, the classic bagpipe and hurdy-gurdy sound.

67 of the 70 presets load in Poly mode and 3 in Mono mode, so you can play chords or single-note lines from the first preset.

Built for Oriental and Rai melodies, Afrotrap and Drill plucked hooks, cinematic Trap intros, medieval and world music, folk-flavoured Hip-Hop loops and Deep House toplines.

  • 8 synthesis engines — Cabrette (bagpipe), Vielle (hurdy-gurdy), Galoubet (pipe), Mandoline, Guitare, Accordeon, Choeur (choir) and Carillon (bells).

FAQ

Which DAWs does it work in?
It is a VST3 instrument on Windows and macOS, plus Audio Unit on macOS. It is designed for FL Studio and should load in any host that supports these formats.

Does it use samples?
No. Every sound is generated in real time by one of eight synthesis engines.

What does the DRONE control do?
It adds a softened saw one octave below each note you play, on every engine. It follows the melody and stops with the note.

Is there a filter envelope?
No. Shape the sound over time with the volume envelope and the LFO.

Can I save my own presets?
Yes. The disk icon saves a .occpreset file, and your presets appear in the User submenu of the preset menu.

Does it add latency?
No. The plugin reports 0 samples of latency.

Is the delay tempo-synced?
No. Delay time is set in seconds (0.05 to 1 s) and the LFO runs in Hz (0.05 to 20 Hz).
