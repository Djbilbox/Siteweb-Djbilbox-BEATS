Stadium-size subs, slap & pluck, reese growls and horns — 70 presets from 8 engines.

ARENA BASS generates every sound in real time — no samples. Choose one of eight engines, shape it with five character controls, then run it through a multimode filter, envelopes, an LFO, distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

Bass presets load in mono for clean one-note lines; the horn bank plays chords with up to 16 voices.

Built for Trap, Drill and Hip-Hop 808s, Afrotrap and Afro bass, G-Funk slides, Deep House and acid lines.

Key features

  • 8 synthesis engines — Sine sub with pitch knock, slap, analog-style mono bass, stadium reese, FM growl, acid rubber, horns and kick.
  • 5 character controls — TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE reshape each engine.
  • 70 factory presets — Seven banks of ten sounds, from 808 subs to stadium horns.
  • Built-in effects — Distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.
  • Play it right away — On-screen keyboard, pitch bend and mod wheel.
  • Royalty-free — Everything you make with it is yours.
