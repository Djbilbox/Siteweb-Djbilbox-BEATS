## Summary (one line)
An 80s synthwave synthesizer with two oscillators, a neon-lit animated interface and 70 ready-to-play presets — VST3, AU and standalone for Windows and Mac.

## Description

**NEON SYNTH 80s 2.0** brings back the sound of night drives, neon skylines and VHS soundtracks: wide supersaw leads, punchy analog-style basses, lush pads, synth brass and sparkling plucks — ready to play the moment you open it.

### What's new in 2.0
- **Second oscillator** — Sawtooth, Square, Triangle, Sine or Pulse 25%, transposed ±24 semitones and fine-tuned for fat detuned stacks, octaves and fifths.
- **70 factory presets** in 6 banks: Leads, Basses, Pads, Keys & Brass, Plucks, FX.
- **New animated interface** — photo-real night-drive scene, machined metal knobs, live oscilloscope, LED output meter and activity lights that react to what you play.
- Installs **next to version 1.0**: your old projects keep working.

### Sound engine
- OSC 1: Sawtooth, Square, Triangle, Sine, SuperSaw (7 voices), 80s Brass
- 5 filter types: LowPass 24 dB, LowPass 12 dB, BandPass, HighPass, Acid LowPass — with resonance, envelope and drive
- Amp envelope with velocity to level and to envelope
- LFO with 6 shapes, 6 targets and its own envelope; LFO-to-pitch vibrato switch
- Poly 8, Poly 16, Mono Retrig and Mono Legato with glide
- Effects: distortion, 80s stereo chorus, tape delay, reverb, output limiter
- Save and load your own presets

### Formats & requirements
- **Windows** 10/11 64-bit — VST3 + standalone app (installer included)
- **macOS** 10.15 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel — VST3, Audio Unit + standalone app
- Works in FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig…
