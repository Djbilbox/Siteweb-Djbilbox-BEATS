Stereo delay with BPM sync, filtered feedback, ducking, ping-pong and four morphing snapshots.

HUMPIRE LAG is a stereo delay with independent left and right times (1 ms to 2 s) or tempo-synced divisions from 1/16 to 1/1 including dotted and triplet values. Feedback goes up to 95% and passes through a low-cut and a high-cut filter in the loop.

Ducking lowers the echoes while you play, ping-pong sends the repeats across the stereo field, and soft saturation in the feedback path keeps long feedback under control.

Four snapshots (A to D) store the time, feedback, filters, ducking and mix. Pick one and the delay morphs to it over 20 ms to 3 s.

Key features

  • Independent L/R times — 1 ms to 2 s, or synced to the host tempo.
  • 8 sync divisions — 1/16, 1/8, dotted 1/8, triplet 1/8, 1/4, dotted 1/4, 1/2 and 1/1.
  • Filtered feedback — Low cut 20 Hz to 1 kHz and high cut 1 to 20 kHz inside the loop.
  • Ducking — Echoes duck while the input plays.
  • Ping-pong — Repeats bounce between left and right.
  • 4 morphing snapshots — Store and recall A to D, with a morph time of 20 ms to 3 s.
  • Preset manager with A/B — Factory presets grouped by category, user presets saved by name, A/B compare, undo and redo.
  • Resizable interface — Scales from 50% to 200% with the proportions locked. Simple and Clean display modes.

FAQ

Which DAWs does it work in?
It is a VST3 effect on Windows and macOS, plus Audio Unit on macOS. It is designed for FL Studio and should load in any host that supports these formats.

Does the sync follow my host tempo?
Yes, when SYNC is on, the delay reads the tempo from the host. Without a tempo from the host it assumes 120 BPM.

Can I save my own presets?
Yes. The SAVE button stores the current settings under a name you choose, and they appear in the User category of the preset menu.
