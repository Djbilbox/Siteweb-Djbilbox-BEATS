What's included

  • HUMPIRE CLUTCH — Compressor with Clean, Punch and Crush modes, sidechain high-pass, parallel mix and a LED life bar.
  • HUMPIRE COMBO — Stutter, tape stop, filter sweep and reverse on two XY pads, with MIDI learn.
  • HUMPIRE GLITCH — Bitcrusher from 1 to 16 bits with sample-rate reduction, speaker filter and dither.
  • HUMPIRE HARDCAP — Look-ahead true-peak limiter with auto-release and EBU R128 LUFS meters.
  • HUMPIRE LAG — Stereo delay with BPM sync, filtered feedback, ducking, ping-pong and four morphing snapshots.
  • HUMPIRE OVERCLOCK — Enhancer for density, harmonics and loudness, with a Power knob and a two-band split.
  • HUMPIRE RESPAWN — Algorithmic reverb with an 8-line FDN, pre-delay, damping, built-in EQ and ducking.
  • HUMPIRE SKILLTREE — Six-band EQ with Mid/Side, minimum or linear phase and a real-time FFT analyzer.
