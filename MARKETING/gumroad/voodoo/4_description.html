Flutes, sitar, oud, kalimba, erhu and a voodoo choir: 70 presets from 8 engines.

VOODOO, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of eight engines (Flute, Pan Flute, Shakuhachi, Sitar, Oud, Kalimba, Erhu and Voodoo Choir), shape it with five character controls (BREATH, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE), then run it through a multimode filter, a volume envelope, an LFO, distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb. The character is mystic and worldly, from Indian and Arabic strings to African kalimba and Andean flutes.

62 of the 70 presets load in Poly mode and 8 in Mono mode, so you can play chords or single-note lines from the first preset.

Built for world-music melodies, Afrobeat and Latin hooks, Trap and Drill ethnic toplines, cinematic and ritual atmospheres.

FAQ

Which DAWs does it work in?
It is a VST3 instrument on Windows and macOS, plus Audio Unit on macOS. It is designed for FL Studio and should load in any host that supports these formats.

Does it use samples?
No. Every sound is generated in real time by one of eight synthesis engines.

Is there a filter envelope?
No. Shape the sound over time with the volume envelope and the LFO.

Is there a velocity control?
Velocity always affects the level of each note. There is no velocity-sensitivity control in this plugin.

Can I save my own presets?
Yes. The disk icon saves a .voodoopreset file, and your presets appear in the User submenu of the preset menu.

Does it add latency?
No. The plugin reports 0 samples of latency.

Is the delay tempo-synced?
No. Delay time is set in seconds (0.05 to 1 s) and the LFO runs in Hz (0.05 to 20 Hz).
