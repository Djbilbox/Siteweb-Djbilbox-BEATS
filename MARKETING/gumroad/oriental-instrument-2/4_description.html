The oriental orchestra, rebuilt from the ground up.

FENNEC puts ten instruments of Arabic and Turkish music in your DAW: oud, saz, qanun, violin, rababah, ney, mizmar, darbuka, riq and bendir. Pick a scale and the plug-in plays real quarter tones for you. Slide between notes like a real player. Choose the look that fits your studio.

This is a new product, not an update: a new sound engine, new instruments, new presets and a new interface.

Key features

  • 10 instruments — Oud, saz, qanun, violin, rababah, ney, mizmar, darbuka, riq, bendir. Warm, clean sound with no harsh highs; every note is measured and tuned.
  • 330 presets with clear names — Oud, Tremolo Oud, Lo-Fi Qanun, Pizzicato Violin, Wedding Mizmar, Sad Ney… sorted by family (Plucked Strings, Bowed Strings, Winds, Drums, Bands, Layers), with search. Every preset is played differently, not just with more echo.
  • Sound design — Body size, synth filter, 808 sub, chop, slides and sample start turn each recording into many different instruments: Bass Oud, Slide Oud, Synth Oud, 808 Oud, Glass Oud…
  • Playing character — Risha tremolo on the plucked strings, trills, slides, breath, a second voice a third above, octave and fifth doubling, ensemble (a second detuned player), lo-fi tape and vinyl, drive, and four violin articulations (arco, pizzicato, tremolo, spiccato).
  • Real quarter tones — 16 Arabic and Turkish scales (Rast, Bayati, Saba, Hijaz, Nahawand, Kurd…). Pick a scale and a key: the right notes are retuned.
  • Scale Lock — Every key plays a note of the scale. No wrong notes.
  • Second voice — A third above or below (always inside the scale), an octave up or down, a fifth, or both octaves.
  • Poly, Mono and Legato — Real glides between notes, natural vibrato that fades in, vibrato on the mod wheel. Pitch and mod wheels on screen.
  • Bands and Layers — 15 ready-made bands (Chaabi Band, Cairo Orchestra, Wedding Band…) and 17 layers that stack two or three instruments (Oud + Qanun, Violin + Ney…).
  • Phrases and drum patterns — Ready-made phrases synced to your tempo (drag the MIDI into your DAW) and a 16-step darbuka and riq sequencer (Maqsum, Saidi, 6/8…).
  • Sound chain — Warmth, Bass, Mid, Treble, Punch, Chorus, Stereo, Reverb and Echo.
  • 5 skins — FIRE, ICE, ROYAL GOLD, WALNUT and SAHARA NIGHT, each with its own layout and animated effect (embers, snow, gold dust, stars).
  • Multi mode — Play up to 4 instruments at once on separate keyboard zones.
  • Your own samples — Load your own SFZ recordings; they take priority over the built-in instruments.

Real recordings. All ten instruments are built from real recordings: qanun, oud, saz, ney, rababah and mizmar from musicians in Istanbul, Fez, Tetouan and Genoa, cut note by note, cleaned and tuned; a solo violin with four articulations; real darbuka, frame drums and tambourines. No Kontakt needed.
