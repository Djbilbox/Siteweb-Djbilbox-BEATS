G-funk whistles, Moog-style leads, talkbox, funk bass and West Coast keys: 70 presets from 8 engines.

SAN ANDREAS LEAD, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of eight engines (G-Funk, Moog, Square, Talkbox, Bass, Rhodes, Strings and Clav), shape it with five character controls (TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE), then run it through a multimode filter, an amplitude envelope, an LFO, distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb. The character is sunny and smooth, built around gliding mono leads.

22 of the 70 presets load in Poly mode and 48 in Mono mode, so you can play chords or single-note lines from the first preset.

Built for G-Funk and West Coast Hip-Hop, funk and R&B hooks, Trap leads and lowrider-style bass lines.

FAQ

Which DAWs does it work in?
It is a VST3 instrument on Windows and macOS, plus Audio Unit on macOS. It is designed for FL Studio and should load in any host that supports these formats.

Does it use samples?
No. Every sound is generated in real time by one of eight synthesis engines.

Can I play chords?
Most lead, talkbox and bass presets load in Mono with glide. Switch VOICE MODE to Poly for chords; the West Coast Keys and Lowrider Strings banks already load in Poly.

Can I save my own presets?
Yes. The disk icon saves a .sapreset file, and your presets appear in the User submenu of the preset menu.

Does it add latency?
No. The plugin reports 0 samples of latency.

Is the delay tempo-synced?
No. Delay time is set in seconds (0.05 to 1 s) and the LFO runs in Hz (0.05 to 20 Hz).
