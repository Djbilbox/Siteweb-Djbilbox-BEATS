80s synthwave and darksynth synth with 6 waveforms, a ladder filter and 70 presets.

K2000, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of six waveforms (Sawtooth, Square, Triangle, Sine, SuperSaw and 80s Brass), shape it with a filter that has five modes, including a 24 dB ladder and an Acid low pass, then add the master effects: distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

An LFO with its own ADSR envelope can move cutoff, pitch, pulse width, volume, chorus or pan. Pitch bend and modulation wheels, glide and four voice modes (Poly 8, Poly 16, Mono Retrig and Mono Legato) cover chords, leads and bass lines, with up to 16 voices.

70 factory presets in seven banks cover leads, basses, pads, plucks and arps, keys and brass, and FX. Built for Synthwave, Darksynth, Retrowave, Synthpop and Outrun productions.

FAQ

Which DAWs does it work in?
It is a VST3 instrument on Windows and macOS, plus Audio Unit on macOS. It is designed for FL Studio and should load in any host that supports these formats.

Does it use samples?
No. Every sound is generated in real time by the oscillator, filter, envelopes and LFO.

Can I play chords?
Yes. Poly 8 (the default) plays up to 8 notes and Poly 16 plays up to 16. The two Mono modes play one note at a time.

Can I save my own presets?
Yes. The disk icon saves a .k2preset file, and your presets appear in the User submenu of the preset menu.

Does it add latency?
No. The plugin reports 0 samples of latency.

Is the delay tempo-synced?
No. Delay time is set in seconds (10 ms to 1 s) and the LFO runs in Hz (0.05 to 25 Hz).
