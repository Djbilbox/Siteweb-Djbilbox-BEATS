Compressor with Clean, Punch and Crush modes, sidechain high-pass, parallel mix and a LED life bar.

HUMPIRE CLUTCH is a feed-forward compressor with a soft knee and a gain-reduction display drawn as a 32-segment life bar that drains as you compress, with a history graph below it.

Clean is a transparent compressor. Punch slows the attack and shortens the release so the transient passes through. Crush multiplies the ratio, speeds up the attack and adds saturation.

A one-pole sidechain high-pass (20 to 500 Hz) keeps low frequencies from triggering the compressor, and the Mix control blends the dry and compressed signals for parallel compression.

Key features

  • 3 modes — Clean, Punch (lets the attack through) and Crush (higher ratio, faster attack, saturation).
  • Full compressor controls — Threshold -40 to 0 dB, ratio 1:1 to 20:1, attack 0.1 to 100 ms, release 10 to 1000 ms, knee 0 to 24 dB.
  • Sidechain high-pass — 20 to 500 Hz, so bass does not over-trigger the detector.
  • Parallel mix — Blend dry and compressed signals from 0 to 100%.
  • LED life bar — A 32-segment bar and a history graph show the gain reduction up to 24 dB.
  • Makeup gain — -12 to +24 dB.
  • Preset manager with A/B — Factory presets grouped by category, user presets saved by name, A/B compare, undo and redo.
  • Resizable interface — Scales from 50% to 200% with the proportions locked. Simple and Clean display modes.

FAQ

Which DAWs does it work in?
It is a VST3 effect on Windows and macOS, plus Audio Unit on macOS. It is designed for FL Studio and should load in any host that supports these formats.

What is the difference between the three modes?
Clean compresses as set. Punch lengthens the attack and shortens the release to keep the transient. Crush raises the ratio, shortens the attack and adds saturation.

Can I save my own presets?
Yes. The SAVE button stores the current settings under a name you choose, and they appear in the User category of the preset menu.
