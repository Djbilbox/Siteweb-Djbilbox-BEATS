OverviewAbout MASTERING

🎚️ MASTERING — a mastering limiter for a loud, clean and competitive master, by DJBILBOX BEATS.

Peak limiting and loudness maximization for your master bus, in a single plugin with every control on one screen.

🎛️ ON THE FRONT PANEL

INPUT: input level fader with L/R meter.

PRE-PROCESS: attack, release and threshold, gain reduction meter and AUTO GAIN.

OUTPUT: harmonics amount, output level and output meter, with AUTO COMP, PEAK HOLD and DITHER settings.

Preset menu with SAVE, plus bypass and compare buttons.

Producers and mixing engineers who want a punchy, polished master on Hip-Hop, Trap, electronic, pop or rock.
