MATRIX MODULAR v1.0.0 — the stereo modulation and auto-pan effect for your West Coast, Hip-Hop and Oriental productions.

If your mix feels flat and static, this is the plugin that opens it up. MATRIX MODULAR is a professional stereo modulation / auto-pan effect built by Djbilbox BEATS, wrapped in a high-tech digital-rain interface. It moves your sound across the stereo field with mathematically clean precision (Equal-Power Pan Law) — no volume dip in the centre.

Made for your beats

West Coast / G-Funk — smooth, sweeping panning on synth leads, whistles and chords.

Oriental / Trap — send oud, violins, ney and heavy percussion travelling across the stereo field for a fully immersive sound.

Lo-Fi / Deep House / Afrotrap — turn loops and FX into a hypnotic tremolo or an aggressive ping-pong chop.

Features

Equal-Power Auto-Pan — flawless stereo field modulation with zero volume drop in the centre.

Tempo-syncable LFO — FREE mode, or sync straight to your DAW tempo at 1/4, 1/8 and 1/16.

4 waveforms — sine, square, triangle and sawtooth, from smooth liquid movement to rapid chopping.

4 instant presets

MORPH — ultra-slow 0.5 Hz dreamy stereo width modulation.

SYNC — tempo-synced 1/4 triangle wave for a perfectly timed auto-pan groove.

ECHO — 1/8 square wave for an extreme stereo ping-pong split.

CHAOS — fast 1/16 sawtooth for complex, glitchy digital motion.
