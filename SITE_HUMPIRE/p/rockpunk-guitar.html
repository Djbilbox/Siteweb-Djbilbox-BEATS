OverviewAbout ROCKPUNK GUITAR

Rock'n'roll guitar with two modelled strings and the Urban Slayer amp: 76 presets, no samples.

ROCKPUNK GUITAR, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, is a rock guitar you play from MIDI. Each note runs two modelled strings (a rhythm string A and a lead string B) that you blend with a layer mix, then goes through the Urban Slayer amp, a drive stage with four flavours (soft, hard, asymmetric and fuzz), a resonant filter, chorus, a tempo-syncable delay and reverb.

It ships no recording: every note is calculated, with up to 8 voices. An optional sampled layer plays your own .wav, .aif or .flac files if you drop them in a folder. The 76 factory presets are grouped in eight families: Crunch rhythm, Leads, High gain, Clean, Bare strings, Nocturnal, Textures and Low end.

Built for rock, punk, rock-flavoured Trap and Drill guitars, riffs, power chords and lead lines.

Key features

Two modelled strings per note — A rhythm string and a lead string, blended with a layer mix, each with tone, body, sustain and pick controls.

Urban Slayer amp — Gain, distortion, tone and level, with an asymmetric saturation stage.

4 drive types — Soft, hard, asymmetric and fuzz.

76 factory presets — Eight families: crunch rhythm, leads, high gain, clean, bare strings, nocturnal, textures and low end.

Tempo-syncable delay — Free time or synced to the host tempo, with feedback and a low-pass in the loop.

Filter, LFO, chorus and reverb — Low-pass, band-pass or high-pass filter, a four-shape LFO, chorus and reverb.

Your own samples (optional) — Drop .wav, .aif or .flac files in a folder to play them on the keyboard. None are included.

FAQ

Which DAWs does it work in?

It is a VST3 instrument on Windows and macOS, plus Audio Unit on macOS. It is designed for FL Studio and should load in any host that supports these formats.

Does it contain guitar recordings?

No. Every note is calculated by the two modelled strings. An optional layer plays your own audio files, and the factory presets do not need it.
