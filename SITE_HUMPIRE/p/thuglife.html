💯 NOT ANOTHER SYNTH — A REAL SAMPLER 💯

Every preset sits on its own sampled instrument — 277 of them — read straight off disk, note by note, without loading the whole bank into memory. Underneath: low-pass, band-pass and high-pass filter, ADSR envelope, an LFO you can point at pitch, cutoff, volume or pan, unison up to 7 voices, portamento, and a distortion → chorus → delay → reverb chain. Five synth waveforms too, if you want to start from nothing. The keyboard lights up red with velocity — like a street signal 🚦
