Neon supersaws, PWM, glitch FM, reese and bit-crushed FX: 70 presets from 8 engines.

CYBERPUNK LEAD, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of eight engines, shape it with five character controls (TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH, TEXTURE), then run it through a multimode filter with its own envelope, an amplitude envelope, an LFO and a master effects chain: distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

The 70 factory presets are organized in seven banks of ten. 32 load in Mono mode for clean single-note leads and basses; the other 38 load in Poly mode, which plays up to 16 voices for chords, pads and arps.

Built for Synthwave and Retrowave, Trap and Drill leads, Hip-Hop hooks, Afrotrap melodies, G-Funk portamento leads and dark electronic bass lines.

Key features

8 synthesis engines — Neon saw, chrome PWM, glitch FM, reese bass, laser pluck, neon brass, data crush and hollow glass.

5 character controls — TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE reshape each engine.

70 factory presets — Seven banks of ten sounds, from neon leads to bit-crushed FX.

Filter with its own envelope — Low pass, high pass or band pass, with resonance and a dedicated ADSR.

Built-in effects — Distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

LFO with 5 shapes — Sine, triangle, saw, square or sample and hold, sent to pitch, filter, amplitude or timbre.

Play it right away — On-screen keyboard (MIDI notes 48 to 96), pitch bend and mod wheel, up to 16 voices in Poly mode.
