OverviewAbout SAN ANDREAS LEAD

G-funk whistles, Moog-style leads, talkbox, funk bass and West Coast keys: 70 presets from 8 engines.

SAN ANDREAS LEAD, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of eight engines (G-Funk, Moog, Square, Talkbox, Bass, Rhodes, Strings and Clav), shape it with five character controls (TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE), then run it through a multimode filter, an amplitude envelope, an LFO, distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb. The character is sunny and smooth, built around gliding mono leads.

22 of the 70 presets load in Poly mode and 48 in Mono mode, so you can play chords or single-note lines from the first preset.

Built for G-Funk and West Coast Hip-Hop, funk and R&B hooks, Trap leads and lowrider-style bass lines.

Moog, Rhodes and Clav are trademarks of their owners and are used only to describe a sound style.

Key features

8 synthesis engines — G-funk whistle, Moog-style lead, PWM square lead, vowel talkbox, funk bass, Rhodes-style FM piano, lowrider strings and clav.

5 character controls — TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE reshape each engine.

70 factory presets — Seven banks of ten sounds, from G-funk whistles and Moog-style leads to talkbox, funk bass, West Coast keys and lowrider strings.

Filter with its own envelope — Low pass, high pass or band pass, with resonance and a dedicated attack, decay, sustain and release envelope.

Built-in effects — Distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

LFO with 5 shapes — Sine, triangle, saw, square or sample and hold, sent to pitch, filter, amplitude or timbre.

Play it right away — On-screen keyboard (MIDI notes 48 to 96), pitch bend and mod wheel, up to 16 voices in Poly mode.
