🥁 HOW IT WORKS

GENERATE: every click gives a new beat (1 to 8 bars) that loops like a real groove, with phrase-end variations and fills.

Per-lane tools: ON, SOLO, LOCK and re-roll (dice) on every lane. Keep the kick you love and re-roll only the hats.

REAL DRUMMER mode: 303 grooves played by real drummers, with their micro-timing and velocities, picked in the style closest to your genre.

Groove controls: swing grid 1/16 or 1/8, swing, density, variation, humanize, fills, speed.

Built-in kit: 8 lanes (kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, perc, tom, shaker) with 4 sounds per lane. You can also drive your own drums through the GM MIDI output.

DRAG MIDI & DRAG AUDIO: drop the pattern as MIDI or as a 24-bit WAV straight into FL Studio, Ableton Live, Studio One, Reaper and more.

Grid editing: add or remove hits and set accents, velocity, probability and ratchets.

🎛️ 883 FACTORY PRESETS

36 genres × 6 flavours (Groove, Variation, Rolls, Night, Minimal, Festive) × 4 variations, each with its own pattern, key and tempo, plus 8 "Real Drummer" presets. The preset browser has search, tags and favourites, and you also get A/B compare and undo/redo.
