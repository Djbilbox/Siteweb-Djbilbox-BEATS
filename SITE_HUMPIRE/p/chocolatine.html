🍫🎚️ CHOCOLATINE is the bus glue compressor from HUMPIRE by DJBILBOX BEATS (Toulouse), with harmonic saturation, transient shaping and stereo width in the same plugin. Put it on your drum bus, your beat or your master to glue everything together. It ships with 875 factory presets tuned for 36 genres.

🎚️ THE CONTROLS

BUTTER: glue amount (compressor threshold, −40 to 0 dB).

LAYERS: release, from 50 ms to 1.2 s.

CHOCO: harmonic saturation, and MELT to blend odd and even harmonics.

CRUNCH: transient contour, less or more attack.

WARMTH: tone tilt from bright to warm.

TLS WIDTH: mid / side stereo width, from mono to 200 %.

GENERATE: a musical random setting when you want a fresh starting point.

IN / OUT meters with peak values, click-free bypass, A/B compare and undo/redo.
