OverviewAbout MPC 2026

A 16-pad beat machine for your DAW and as a standalone app. Every control on screen is real: the pads light up as they play, the knobs turn, and the processing actually shapes the sound.

IMPORTANT — please read before downloading: MPC 2026 does not include a sample bank. It plays the samples already installed on your computer (FL Studio's library in particular) and lays them out across the pads as 50 ready-to-play kits, each with its own groove.

WHAT YOU GET

16 beat pads, playable by mouse, computer keyboard, or MIDI notes 36-51

  • 50 kits: 10 styles x 5 variations (G-Funk, Boom Bap, Trap, Drill, House, Techno, Lo-Fi, Afro, Electro, Reggaeton)
  • 12 pad-volume knobs, paged across the 16 pads
  • Attack, Decay and Reverb: top row shapes pads 1-8, bottom row pads 9-16
  • Sequencer locked to your project tempo and playhead
  • Keyboard mapping: 1234 / AZER / QSDF / WXCV, spacebar runs the sequencer

FORMATS

Windows 10/11 64-bit: VST3 + standalone app

  • macOS: Audio Unit + VST3 + standalone app
  • User manual (PDF, French) included

How it is built

Written in C++ with the JUCE framework, compiled in Visual Studio on Windows and version-controlled on GitHub. The interface is designed in Figma. No generative AI is used in the code, the sound engine or the artwork.

Royalty-free for your own productions.
