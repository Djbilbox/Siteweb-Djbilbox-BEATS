Golden leads, brass, pads, plucks, bells and basses: 70 presets from 8 synthesis engines.

GOLDHAWK, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of eight engines, shape it with five character controls (TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH, TEXTURE), then run it through a multimode filter with its own envelope, an amplitude envelope, an LFO, distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

The 70 factory presets are organized in seven banks of ten: Hawk Leads, Golden Brass, Royal Pads, Falcon Plucks, Sky Bells & Strings, Talon Bass and Eagle Cry & FX. 43 presets load in Poly mode, which plays up to 16 voices; 27 load in Mono mode for single-note leads, basses and cries.

Built for epic and cinematic beats, anthem leads, brass hits, atmospheric pads, bell and pluck motifs, string beds, bass lines and risers.

Key features

8 synthesis engines — Hawk lead, golden brass, royal pad, falcon pluck, FM sky bells, feather strings, talon bass and eagle cry.

5 character controls — TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE reshape each engine.

70 factory presets — Seven banks of ten sounds, from anthem leads to eagle cries.

Filter with its own envelope — Low pass, high pass or band pass, with resonance and a dedicated attack, decay/release and sustain envelope.

Built-in effects — Distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

LFO with 5 shapes — Sine, triangle, saw, square or sample and hold, sent to pitch, filter, amplitude or timbre.

Play it right away — On-screen keyboard (MIDI notes 36 to 96), pitch bend and mod wheel, up to 16 voices in Poly mode.
