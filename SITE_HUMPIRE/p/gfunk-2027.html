OverviewAbout GFUNK 2027

GFUNK 2027 by HUMPIRE by Djbilbox BEATS is a synthesizer made for whistle leads, sub-boogie basses, talkbox-style pads and lowrider brass — the sounds that define West Coast G-Funk — wrapped in a lowrider-inspired interface with the man, the woman and the Impala popping out of the panel in 3D.

What you get

1000 factory presets in 10 categories: Whistle Leads, Synth Leads, Sub Bass, Boogie Bass, Talkbox Vocal, Pads, Keys, Brass Stabs, Plucks & Bells, Arps & Sequences — with a category browser and favourites.

8-voice engine: two oscillators with a sine to triangle to saw to square morph, sub oscillator, noise, 1–7 voice unison, multimode filter (LP12 / LP24 / band / high-pass), two ADSR envelopes, two LFOs.

Poly / Mono / Legato modes with glide — the West Coast whistle slide.

Arpeggiator (up, down, up-down, random) synced to your DAW tempo.

Effects: tape saturation and wobble, phaser, chorus, tempo-synced ping-pong delay, reverb.

Big LED screen: oscilloscope, preset browser, 64-band spectrum analyser.

6 complete skins: Rose Plastic, Gold Vault, Neon Midnight, Candy Blue, Carbon Red, Walnut Cream.

Light show: marquee lights, glowing keys, sparkles and panel pulses that follow your playing.

Resizable window, pitch and mod wheels, sustain pedal support.

Windows installer (VST3 + standalone app). The macOS version (AU + VST3 + standalone) is coming soon.

Requirements

Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) — any VST3 host (FL Studio, Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper, Bitwig…)

4 GB RAM, screen of 1280 × 720 or more
