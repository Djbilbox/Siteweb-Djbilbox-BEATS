Pick a genre, hit GENERATE and get a bassline that follows the chords, then shape the sound from round sub to wide reese or acid. It covers 36 genres and ships with 877 factory presets.

🎛️ THE SYNTH

ENGINE oscillator with OSC 2, SUB (sine or triangle) and noise.

Filter: low-pass, band-pass or high-pass with ALTITUDE (cutoff), RESO and ENV AMT, plus DESERT DRIVE saturation before the filter.

WINGS: unison width and detune (1 to 3 voices), and HEAT for soft output saturation.

GLIDE with Mono, Legato or Poly voice modes.

Modulation: amp and filter envelopes, 2 LFOs (sine, triangle, saw, square, S&H) and a 2-slot mod matrix (LFOs, filter envelope, velocity, mod wheel).

Oversampling up to 4x for clean drive.

🎲 THE BASSLINE GENERATOR

GENERATE: 2-bar bass riffs that follow the chord progression, with approach notes.

Key, scale, bars, octave and density; the settings reshape the pattern live.

DRAG MIDI straight into FL Studio, Ableton Live, Studio One, Reaper and more, with grid editing for notes, velocity, accents and probability.

🎛️ 877 FACTORY PRESETS

36 genres × 6 flavours (Round Bass, Pluck Bass, Wide Reese, Acid, Clean Sub, Dirty Growl) × 4 variations, each with its own bassline, key and tempo. The preset browser has search, tags and favourites, and you also get A/B compare and undo/redo.
