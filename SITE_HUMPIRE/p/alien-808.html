🔊 THE SOUND

UFO: multistage saturation, from clean sub to a distorted 808 that cuts through phone speakers.

GRAVITY + ABDUCTION: depth (up to 48 semitones) and time of the pitch drop, from a subtle punch to a long dive.

MUTATE: morphs the waveform from sine to triangle to wavefolder.

GLOW: asymmetric diode stage for warmth.

SUB, PUNCH, DECAY to shape the body and the tail.

🎲 THE 808 PATTERN GENERATOR

GENERATE: 2-bar 808 riffs that follow the chord progression, with approach notes, slides and glides.

Key, scale and bars on the left; the settings reshape the pattern live.

DRAG MIDI straight into FL Studio, Ableton Live, Studio One, Reaper and more, with grid editing for notes, velocity, accents and probability.

🎛️ 876 FACTORY PRESETS

36 genres × 6 flavours (808 Glide, 808 Punch, Dirty 808, Deep Sub, Short 808, Mutant 808) × 4 variations, each with its own pattern, key and tempo. The preset browser has search, tags and favourites, and you also get A/B compare and undo/redo.
