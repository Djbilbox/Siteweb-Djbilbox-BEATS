🦍🥁 BLOCK GORILLA is the 16-pad drum machine from HUMPIRE by DJBILBOX BEATS (Toulouse), with a sampler, a sequencer and a beat generator in one plugin. It covers 36 genres, ships with 881 factory presets and includes grooves played by real drummers.

🥁 THE DRUM MACHINE

16 pads with per-pad GAIN, PAN, PITCH, FILTER, DECAY and CHOKE.

Built-in synthesized kit, and you can load your own WAV / AIFF samples on any pad.

Patterns A to D: CHAIN them into a full beat and EXPORT WAV.

FX BLOCK: SMASH, KNOCK, RAGE, TRASH, DUST and ROOM to rough up your drums.

MIDI: pad 1 = C1 (note 36), so it plays from any keyboard or pad controller.

🎲 THE BEAT GENERATOR

GENERATE: a new beat per click, in the genre you pick.

Per-lane tools: ON, SOLO, LOCK and re-roll on every lane. Keep what works and re-roll the rest.

REAL DRUMMER mode: 303 grooves played by real drummers, with their micro-timing and velocities, picked in the style closest to your genre.

DRAG MIDI & DRAG AUDIO: drop the beat as MIDI or as a 24-bit WAV straight into your DAW.

🎛️ 881 FACTORY PRESETS

36 genres × 6 flavours (Block, Street, Vinyl, Stadium, Rage, Minimal) × 4 variations, each with its own beat, plus 6 "Real Drummer" presets. The preset browser has search, tags and favourites, and you also get A/B compare and undo/redo.

🎁 BONUS: HUMPIRE MIDI DRUMS PACK

360 drum MIDI files covering the 36 genres, ready to drop on BLOCK GORILLA or any drum plugin.

💻 FORMATS

Windows 10/11 64-bit: VST3 + Standalone app

macOS 11 or later, Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel): VST3

Works in any VST3 host (FL Studio, Ableton Live, Studio One, Reaper, Bitwig, Cubase…). No AU / AAX version.

Interface in French or English, resizable from 75 to 200 %.

Automated tests render every preset, and the audio thread never allocates memory. The plugin passes pluginval (strictness 8) on Windows and macOS.
