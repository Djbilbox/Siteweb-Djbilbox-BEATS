OverviewAbout STATION SYNTH

🌍 4,128 presets. 44 wavetables. 11 libraries. One synthesizer.

STATION SYNTH is a complete wavetable synthesizer: two morphing oscillators, sub, noise, multimode filter, modulation matrix, three envelopes, tempo-synced LFOs and a seven-effect rack (distortion, compressor, EQ, chorus, delay, reverb, limiter).

📦 WHAT YOU GET

4,128 ready-to-play presets, sorted by category: BASS, LEAD, PLUCK, PAD, KEYS.

44 wavetables built from real timbres: human voice, saxophone, cello, violin, electric piano, organ, clavinet, guitars, double bass, theremin, console chips.

11 country libraries: FRANCE, ENGLAND, USA, BRAZIL, ESPAGNE, ALGERIE, NIGERIA, JAMAIQUE, JAPON, PORTO RICO, plus PASSPORT. Every preset is named after a city, and each library brings three exclusive wavetables.

5 skins: CLASSIC, WOOD, GOLD, DIAMOND, NOIR — one click changes the front panel.

💻 FORMATS

Windows: VST3 + Standalone, .exe installer that puts everything in place.

macOS: VST3 + Audio Unit (Logic, GarageBand) + Standalone app, Universal binary (Intel & Apple Silicon).
