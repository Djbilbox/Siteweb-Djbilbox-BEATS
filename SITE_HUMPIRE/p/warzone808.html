808 subs, reese, leads, plucks, pads and war FX: 60 factory presets plus a 10-preset expansion.

WARZONE808, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, is a synthesizer built around the 808: it opens in Mono mode with last-note priority and glide between held notes, so slides sound right from the first preset. Pick one of five waveforms (sine, square, sawtooth, triangle or noise), stack up to seven detuned voices in unison, and shape the sound with a resonant filter, two envelopes, an LFO, distortion, chorus, delay and reverb.

34 of the 60 factory presets load in Mono mode and 26 in Poly mode (up to 16 voices). The factory presets are in six banks of ten: 808 Subs, Dirty & Reese, Leads, Plucks & Keys, Pads & Ambience and FX & Percussion. A built-in Expansion Vol.1 adds ten more presets, and the EXPANSION cassette loads additional .wz808exp packs.

Built for Trap, Drill and Hip-Hop 808s, reese and growl bass, leads, pads, risers and war-themed sound effects.

Key features

5 oscillator waveforms — Sine, square, sawtooth, triangle and noise.

7-voice unison — Up to seven detuned copies per note, with detune up to 60 cents and stereo spread.

70 presets — 60 factory presets in six banks of ten, plus the built-in Expansion Vol.1 with ten more.

Resonant filter with envelope — Low pass, band pass or high pass, resonance up to Q 20 and a filter envelope of up to 8 octaves.

Mono legato glide — Mono mode keeps a held-note stack with last-note priority and glides between notes without retriggering.

Built-in effects — Distortion, chorus, delay and reverb, with extra hidden controls available to automation.

Expansion packs — Load extra presets from .wz808exp files with the EXPANSION cassette.

Royalty-free — Everything you make with it is yours.
