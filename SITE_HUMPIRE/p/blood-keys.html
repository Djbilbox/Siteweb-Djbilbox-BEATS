🎃 HAUNTED FAIRGROUND PIANO

BLOOD KEYS is a horror piano from HUMPIRE by DJBILBOX BEATS, built like a ghost-train ride. Real recorded pianos and instruments meet synthesised horror textures. The whole faceplate lights up as you play: carnival bulbs, flickering pumpkins, glowing eyes, blood-red keys and LEDs.

🩸 SOUNDS

  • 2 layers (A + B) picked from 12 sources: haunted grand piano, old upright, music box, funeral bells, spectral harp, ghost glasses, cello tremolo, gloomy cello, ghost choir, abyss drone, spectral breath, theremin.
  • POSSESSION: every note detunes and drifts on its own. AGONY: notes slowly die in pitch.
  • Filter, LFO, ADSR, drive, lo-fi tape, tempo-synced ping-pong delay, reverb.
  • 4 atmospheres: wind, storm (with lightning), heartbeat synced to your tempo, whispers.
  • 822 presets: cinematic, lo-fi, possessed, reversed, cathedral, Halloween trap.

💡 LIGHT SHOW

The sign's bulbs chase and flash on every note, the two character windows are level meters, and the keys and their LEDs light up with your velocity.

💻 FORMATS

  • Windows 10/11 64-bit: VST3 + Standalone
  • macOS 11+ Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel): VST3
  • FL Studio, Ableton Live, REAPER, Studio One, Cubase, Bitwig… (no AU / Logic for now)

✅ 100% ROYALTY-FREE

Use everything you make with BLOOD KEYS in your beats and songs, commercial or not.
