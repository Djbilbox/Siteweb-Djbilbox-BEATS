Algorithmic reverb with an 8-line FDN, pre-delay, damping, built-in EQ and ducking.

HUMPIRE RESPAWN is an algorithmic reverb built on an eight-line feedback delay network with a Householder matrix, early reflections and a modulated tail. Decay runs from 0.2 to 20 seconds and Size from 0.5 to 2.

Pre-delay (0 to 200 ms), damping, early-reflection level, modulation, low cut (20 Hz to 1 kHz) and high cut (2 to 20 kHz) shape the space. Ducking lowers the reverb while the input is playing, so vocals stay clear.

The factory presets are grouped as Plate, Hall, Hammam and Cathedral spaces plus instrument-specific rooms.

Key features

8-line FDN — Eight delay lines with a Householder feedback matrix.

Decay 0.2 to 20 s — With size, pre-delay and damping controls.

Built-in EQ — Low cut 20 Hz to 1 kHz and high cut 2 to 20 kHz on the reverb.

Ducking — The reverb ducks while the input plays.

Modulation — Slow modulation of the delay lines for a smoother tail.

Space presets — Plate, Hall, Hammam and Cathedral categories.

Preset manager with A/B — Factory presets grouped by category, user presets saved by name, A/B compare, undo and redo.

Resizable interface — Scales from 50% to 200% with the proportions locked. Simple and Clean display modes.

Vocals, Drums, Oud and acoustic, Ambient
