Why MINI MPC HUMPIRE Changes the Game

Forget downloading sample packs, checking licenses, or eating up gigabytes of storage. MINI MPC HUMPIRE computes every sound on the fly: open the plugin, tap a pad, it sounds — instantly, on any machine, identically every time.

The moment it opens, a factory kit of 16 sounds (kick, snare, clap, hats, cymbals, toms, cowbell, shaker, conga, sub 808, zap) is ready to play, whether you're using a MIDI keyboard or your mouse.

Loading a kit automatically sets your project tempo to that style's tempo (unless your DAW is imposing its own).

Over 1000 Patterns, Generated in Real Time

Each of the 13 styles ships with roughly 75 to 80 hand-authored, procedurally-varied rhythm patterns (kick/snare/hat placement, swing, ghost notes, fills) — adding up to more than 1000 grooves across the whole plugin, all composed and rendered in real time by the built-in rhythm engine. No external samples, no royalty-free packs, nothing to clear: everything you hear is generated by MINI MPC HUMPIRE itself.

The PATTERN BROWSE function (SHIFT + SEQ) unlocks up to 64 patterns per style, organized across 4 pages of 16 via the PAD BANK fader.

A Real Production Workflow, Not Just a Sample Player

Three context-aware knobs (K1/K2/K3) controlling Start/End/Loop, Level/Pan/Tune, Attack/Decay/Release, filter/drive, delay, and more.

CHOP: automatically slices a sample across the 16 pads of a bank (transient-based or equal slices).

FLEX BEAT: humanizes your loops with subtle randomized timing and velocity.

NOTE REPEAT, TRIPLET, 16 LEVELS, STEP EDIT for sculpting sequences like on a classic MPC.

RECALL / SAMPLE RECORD / SAVE SAMPLE: the plugin keeps the last 20 seconds of its own output in memory at all times — grab and export on the fly.

Native host sync: follows your DAW's tempo and position with no drift, detachable anytime.

Built to Stay Light

Factory sounds are recomputed on every load, so they never add weight to your project. Only the kit, the 16 sequences, tempo, and pad settings get saved — your session files stay lean, even with hundreds of patterns in use.

System Requirements

VST3 or Audio Unit (macOS), or standalone application use

MIDI keyboard compatible (pad A01 = note 36 / C1, up to note 99 across the 4 banks)
