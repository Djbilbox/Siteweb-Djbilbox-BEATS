OverviewAbout HUMPIRE OVERCLOCK

Enhancer for density, harmonics and loudness, with a Power knob and a two-band split.

HUMPIRE OVERCLOCK splits the signal into a low band and a high band at an adjustable frequency (80 Hz to 4 kHz) and saturates each band with 2x oversampling. The low band gets density, the high band gets generated harmonics.

POWER sets the overall drive, CURVE morphs from a soft asymmetric curve to a hard clip, DENSITY mixes the low-band saturation in and HARMONICS sets the high-band harmonics. The output is level-compensated so more power does not mean a much louder signal.

On the screen, a ring and an animated fan follow the Power setting.

Key features

Power knob — One control for the overall drive, with level compensation.

Curve — From a soft, asymmetric curve to a hard clip.

Density and harmonics — Low-band density and high-band harmonics are set separately.

Two-band split — Split frequency from 80 Hz to 4 kHz.

2x oversampling — Saturation runs oversampled to limit aliasing.

Output trim — -18 to +12 dB.

Preset manager with A/B — Factory presets grouped by category, user presets saved by name, A/B compare, undo and redo.

Resizable interface — Scales from 50% to 200% with the proportions locked. Simple and Clean display modes.
