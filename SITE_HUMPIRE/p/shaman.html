OverviewAbout SHAMAN

Didgeridoo, tribal drums, jaw harp, spirit flute, chant choir and earth drones: 70 presets from 8 engines.

SHAMAN, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of eight engines (Didgeridoo, Tribal Drum, Jaw Harp, Spirit Flute, Chant Choir, Fire Pad, Bone Chimes and Earth Drone), shape it with five character controls (TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE), then run it through a multimode filter, an amplitude envelope, an LFO, distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb. The character is earthy and ritual, with breath, growl and slow-moving drones.

46 of the 70 presets load in Poly mode and 24 in Mono mode, so you can play chords or single-note lines from the first preset.

Built for tribal and world-music beats, cinematic and ritual scoring, Afro and Latin percussion layers, dark ambient and Trap intros.

Key features

8 synthesis engines — Growling didgeridoo, pitch-dropping tribal drum, jaw harp, breathy spirit flute, vowel chant choir, fire pad, bone chimes and earth drone.

5 character controls — TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE reshape each engine.

70 factory presets — Seven banks of ten sounds, from didgeridoo and tribal drums to jaw harp, spirit flute, chant choir, fire pads and bone chimes.

Filter with its own envelope — Low pass, high pass or band pass, with resonance and a dedicated attack, decay, sustain and release envelope.

Built-in effects — Distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

LFO with 5 shapes — Sine, triangle, saw, square or sample and hold, sent to pitch, filter, amplitude or timbre.

Play it right away — On-screen keyboard (MIDI notes 36 to 84), pitch bend and mod wheel, up to 16 voices in Poly mode.
