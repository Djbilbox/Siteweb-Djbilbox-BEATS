OverviewAbout EQ-PRO SPIDER

EQ-PRO SPIDER is a free 8-band parametric equaliser from HUMPIRE — DJBILBOX BEATS, wrapped in a dark neon web-themed interface where every knob glows and the display pulses with your music.

Shape your sound right on the live curve: drag a node sideways for frequency, up and down for gain, and roll the mouse wheel for Q. A real-time spectrum analyser shows exactly what you are doing.

Three voicings change the whole character of the EQ — and the colour of the interface:

CLASSIC — clean digital surgery

SYMBIOTE — wider curves and warm asymmetric saturation

IRON-SUIT — tight surgical Q plus a 12 kHz air lift

Completely FREE. No limitations. Windows and Mac.

Features:

8 bands: high-pass (12/24 dB), low shelf, 4 parametric bells, high shelf, low-pass (12/24 dB)

Interactive EQ curve with draggable nodes + real-time spectrum analyser

3 voicings: Classic, Symbiote, Iron-Suit

Mid/Side processing

Built-in 3-band OTT (upward + downward multiband compression) with band focus

Drive, Mix, Input and Output gain

17 factory presets + save your own

A/B compare, undo/redo and a musical randomiser

Resizable interface (60% to 130%)

Royalty-free for your productions

Formats:

Windows 10/11 (64-bit): VST3 + Standalone — .exe installer

macOS 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel): VST3 + Audio Unit + Standalone — .pkg installer

Works in FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, GarageBand, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig and more.

In the download: one ZIP for Windows and one for Mac, each with the installer, a full user manual and the licence.

Note: the installers are not code-signed. On Windows click "More info" then "Run anyway". On Mac, right-click the .pkg and choose Open. Step-by-step instructions are in INSTALL.txt.
