OverviewAbout CASBAH CHORDS

🕌🎹 CASBAH CHORDS is the chord and melody generator from HUMPIRE by DJBILBOX BEATS (Toulouse). Pick a genre and a key, hit GENERATE and drag chords and melodies into your DAW. It covers 36 genres in one plugin and ships with 875 factory presets, from Trap and Drill to Raï, Chaâbi, Jazz and Neo-Soul.

🎼 CHORDS AND MELODIES THAT MAKE SENSE

Voice-led progressions: each chord takes the inversion closest to the previous one, so the progression flows instead of jumping around.

7ths and 9ths where the genre calls for them: Jazz, Neo-Soul / Gospel, R&B, Lo-fi, Funk / Disco, G-Funk and Jazzy Boom Bap.

Melodies with a hook: a 2-bar motif developed across the phrase (A A' B A'), chord tones on the strong beats, stepwise movement and a resolution on the tonic.

Oriental modes: Hijaz, Nahawand and Kurd, plus Bayati and Saba (approximated to semitones), alongside the classic major and minor scales.

Controls: DARBOUKA rhythms, VOICING, STRUM, MELODY and CHORD KEYS.

Built-in mandole sound to preview ideas, and MIDI out on two channels (chords and melody) to drive your own synths.

DRAG MIDI straight into FL Studio, Ableton Live, Studio One, Reaper and more, with grid editing for notes, velocity, accents and probability.

🎛️ 875 FACTORY PRESETS

36 genres × 6 flavours (Chords, Chords + Melody, Wide Pads, Stabs, Mandole Lead, Maqam Groove) × 4 variations, each with its own progression, key and tempo. The preset browser has search, tags and favourites, and you also get A/B compare and undo/redo.

🎁 BONUS: HUMPIRE MIDI CHORDS & MELODIES PACK

432 chord and melody MIDI files covering the 36 genres, ready for any synth or piano.
