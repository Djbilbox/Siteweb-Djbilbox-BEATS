OverviewAbout CHINTAWAZ

Sitar, flower-power keys, tape strings, flutes, kalimba, pads and organ: 70 presets from 8 engines.

CHINTAWAZ, sold under the HUMPIRE brand by DJBILBOX BEATS, generates every sound in real time with no samples. Choose one of eight engines, shape it with five character controls (TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH, TEXTURE), then run it through a multimode filter with its own envelope, an amplitude envelope, an LFO, distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

58 of the 70 presets load in Poly mode, which plays up to 16 voices for chords and pads. The other 12 load in Mono mode for flute leads, tape violin, bass and organ lines.

Built for Lo-fi Hip-Hop and Boom Bap loops, psychedelic Soul and Funk, Oriental-flavored melodies, Afrobeats and Amapiano, and dreamy retro pads.

Key features

8 synthesis engines — Sitar Bloom, Flower EP, Mellotron, Hippie Flute, Kalimba, Daisy Pad, Bloom Bass and Psyche Organ.

5 character controls — TONE, BODY, BRIGHT, WIDTH and TEXTURE reshape each engine.

70 factory presets — Seven banks of ten sounds, from sitar plucks to psychedelic organs.

Filter with its own envelope — Low pass, high pass or band pass, with resonance and a dedicated envelope.

Built-in effects — Distortion, chorus, ping-pong delay and reverb.

LFO with 5 shapes — Sine, triangle, saw, square or sample and hold, sent to pitch, filter, amplitude or timbre.

Play it right away — On-screen keyboard (MIDI notes 48 to 96), pitch bend and mod wheel, up to 16 voices in Poly mode.

Royalty-free — Everything you make with it is yours.
