OverviewAbout LA BAGUETTE

🥖📼 LA BAGUETTE is the tape compressor from HUMPIRE by DJBILBOX BEATS (Toulouse): a compressor and a tape machine in one plugin, for drums, vocals, 808s and full mixes. It ships with 875 factory presets tuned for 36 genres.

🎚️ THE CONTROLS

PEDALE: threshold (−48 to 0 dB).

MICHETTE: ratio (1:1 to 20:1).

BERET and TROMPETTE: attack (0.1 to 120 ms) and release (20 ms to 2 s).

CORNIAUD: tape saturation with drive and magnetic memory.

VADROUILLE: wow & flutter, the tape wobble that makes loops feel old.

BEURRE: parallel dry / wet mix for New York-style compression.

GENERATE: a musical random setting when you want a fresh starting point.

IN / OUT meters with peak values, click-free bypass, A/B compare and undo/redo.
