🔊 BIGBASS — Lowrider Bass Synth by DJBILBOX BEATS

BIGBASS is a bass plugin built to deliver sub-bass, 808s and West Coast basslines with real impact in the mix. It combines 6 bass characters, 80 presets and a polyphonic engine with glide, a 24 dB filter, full ADSR controls and built-in effects — inside an interface inspired by the Los Angeles lowrider scene.

Made for Trap, Afrotrap, Hip-Hop, G-Funk and bass-heavy productions, BIGBASS switches quickly from a clean sub to an aggressive, textured or melodic bass.

🎛️ FEATURES

6 bass modes: SUB, SLAM, PUNCH, GROWL, SCREAM, DOOM.

80 factory presets in three themes: Los Santos, Lakers, Los Angeles.

8-voice polyphony with glide, a 24 dB low-pass filter and a full ADSR envelope.

Built-in reverb, delay and soft distortion.

Integrated virtual keyboard to play without a MIDI controller.

License key activation (one-time activation per machine).

🎧 GENRES

Trap · Afrotrap · Hip-Hop · G-Funk · bass music · melodic productions built on 808s and sub-bass.

💻 COMPATIBILITY

VST3 plugin or Standalone on Windows; VST3 on macOS. Made for VST3 hosts such as FL Studio, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One and Reaper.

🔥 WHY BIGBASS?

Not just clean bass tones: BIGBASS creates basses with character, glide and drive, with ready-to-use presets and direct controls for presence, attack and depth.
