FAQ

Good to know

It's my first time in a studio — is that a problem?

Not at all. Sessions are coached: we warm up, work the flow and build the track step by step.

Can I bring my own instrumental?

Yes. Bring a WAV (and stems if you have them). Make sure you own the right license to release it.

Who owns the recordings?

You do. Your vocals and your masters are yours — we only ask for a production credit when we made the beat.

Can I pay in several times?

Yes, every pack can be paid in instalments. A deposit confirms the booking.
