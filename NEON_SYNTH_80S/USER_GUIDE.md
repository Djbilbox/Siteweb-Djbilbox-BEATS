# NEON SYNTH 80s 2.0 — User Guide

**HUMPIRE by Djbilbox BEATS** · VST3 / AU / Standalone · Windows & macOS

## Install
- **Windows:** run `NEON_SYNTH_80s_2_Setup.exe`. It installs the VST3 in `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3` and the standalone app in `C:\Program Files\HUMPIRE\NEON SYNTH 80s 2.0`. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW.
- **macOS:** open `NEON_SYNTH_80s_2_Mac.pkg` with right-click > Open (see `README_FIRST.txt`).
- NEON SYNTH 80s 2.0 installs next to NEON SYNTH 80s 1.0: both stay available.

## The panel
- **OSC 1** — 6 waveforms (Sawtooth, Square, Triangle, Sine, SuperSaw, 80s Brass), voice mode (Poly 8, Poly 16, Mono Retrig, Mono Legato), Detune, Glide.
- **OSC 2** — Sawtooth, Square, Triangle, Sine or Pulse 25%, transposed ±24 semitones, fine-tuned ±50 cents, mixed in with MIX.
- **FILTER** — LowPass 24 dB, LowPass 12 dB, BandPass, HighPass, Acid LowPass; Cutoff, Resonance, Envelope amount, Drive.
- **AMP ENVELOPE** — Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release; velocity to level and to envelope.
- **LFO** — 6 shapes, 6 targets (Cutoff, Pitch, Pulse Width, Volume, Chorus, Pan), Rate, Amount, its own envelope, LFO > Pitch switch for vibrato.
- **EFFECTS** — Distortion, Chorus, Reverb, Delay (mix, time, feedback), master Volume with output limiter.
- **OUT meter**, oscilloscope and activity LEDs show what the synth is doing.

## Presets
70 factory presets in 6 banks: Leads, Basses, Pads, Keys & Brass, Plucks, FX. Use `<` `>` or **PRESETS** to browse. **SAVE** stores your own sounds in `Documents/DJBILBOX BEATS/NEON SYNTH 80s/Presets` (`.neonpreset` files), listed under **User**.

## Tips
- Drag a knob up/down to change it; the value appears while you drag.
- The window can be resized from the bottom-right corner.
- Mod wheel opens the filter and adds chorus; pitch bend is ±2 semitones.
